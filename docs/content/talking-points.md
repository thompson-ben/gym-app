# Talking to camera: talking points (not scripts)

**How these work:** the only line to say word for word is the **opening line**, because it's the hook. Everything else is a point to talk about in your own words, like you're telling a mate. If a point doesn't feel like you, skip it.

**Before each one:** send yourself a 20-second voice note answering the prompt. Then film straight away.

**Text style:** same as "It's just me". The hook goes in a dark box in the top third. Captions sit over your chest. One word per video is lime. The question at the end goes in a dark box.

---

## A. "5:30am brain" (tired mode) ⭐ start here

**The idea:** you train at 5:30am, when nobody can think, and the app does the thinking for you. It's real, it's relatable, and it shows exactly what the app is for without sounding like an advert.

**Film:** in the car in the gym car park, about 5:20am, still dark. Interior light on, phone propped on the dashboard or held at eye level. Don't fix your hair. The tired look is the point.

| Moment | Say it your way | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening line** (word for word) | *"It's 5:30 in the morning and my brain doesn't work yet."* | **5:30am. Brain: offline.** |
| Point 1 | At this hour you can't remember what you lifted last week. You're not doing maths in your head. | *What did I bench last week? 🤷* |
| Point 2 | That's why you built it this way: open it, tap Start, and last week's numbers are already there beside every set. | |
| App clip (2–3 s) | *(silent, or "Look.")* Tap Start, and the "last time" numbers show. | *Last time: already there* |
| Point 3 | It even tells you when to add weight. You just turn up and beat the number. | Lime: **zero thinking** |
| Walk-off | Open the door: "Right. Let's see if I beat it." | |
| **End question** | *"Are you a morning or an evening trainer?"* | **Morning or evening? 👇** |

**Length:** 25–35 seconds.

**Caption:**
> 5:30am. Brain fully offline 😴
>
> I train before work, and at that hour I can't remember what I lifted last week, never mind do maths between sets. So Setmodo does the thinking: open it, tap Start, and last time's numbers are right there beside every set.
>
> Turn up. Beat the number. Go to work.
>
> Morning or evening trainer? 👇
>
> #5amclub #morningworkout #gymlife #progressiveoverload #gymmotivation

**Part 2 (optional, the same morning):** film 15 seconds in the car afterwards. *"Update: beat bench by one rep. Didn't think once."* Post it as a story that day, or as tomorrow's reel.

## B. "What I trained today" (a repeatable series)

**The idea:** a quick check-in after your session. Did you beat last week or not? Being honest when you don't is just as good content. Make it a named series so people come back for it.

**Series name on screen:** **5:30am club · Day 1** (then Day 2, Day 3…). It ties in with A.

**Film:** in the car straight after training, about 6:45am. Sweaty is fine. Keep it under 30 seconds.

| Moment | Say it your way | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening line** (word for word) | *"Just finished [Push]. Did I beat last week?"* | **5:30am club · Day 1** and **Did I beat last week?** |
| Point 1 | One lift: last week's number, today's number. | *Bench: 60 × 10 → 60 × 11* |
| App clip (2 s) | Show that exercise in the app, last time next to today. | Lime: **+1 rep** |
| Point 2 | How it felt, in one sentence. Easy? Grind? Nearly failed? | |
| Point 3 (if true) | Whether the app says to add weight next time. | *Next time: add weight ✓* |
| **End question** | *"What did you train today?"* | **What did you train? 👇** |

**On bad days:** *"Didn't beat it today. Same as last week. That's fine, I'll get it next time."* People love the honesty, and it still shows the app.

**Caption template** (change the bits in brackets):
> 5:30am club · Day [1] 🌅
>
> [Push] done. Bench went from [60 × 10] to [60 × 11]. One rep. That's how it works: beat last time, every time.
>
> What did you train today? 👇
>
> #5amclub #progressiveoverload #gymlife #pushday #trainingdiary

**How often:** 2–3 times a week. These are your quickest videos to make: film, trim, caption, post in about 10 minutes.

## C. "Rate my split"

**The idea:** you ask people to judge you. Everyone has an opinion on splits, so it gets lots of comments, and it shows the app's split screen naturally.

**Film:** at home, eye level, relaxed. Have your split open in the app ready to screen record.

| Moment | Say it your way | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening line** (word for word) | *"Be honest: rate my training split out of 10."* | **Rate my split /10** |
| Point 1 | What it is, quickly: e.g. "Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, 4 days a week, 5:30 in the morning." | |
| Screen recording (4–5 s) | Scroll slowly through your split in the app, then open one day so the exercises show. | *[Your split name]* |
| Point 2 | Why you set it up that way. Time? What you enjoy? What you want to improve? | |
| Point 3 | The bit you're not sure about. "I'm not sure I'm doing enough for…" This is what gets people commenting. | Lime: **not sure about…** |
| **End question** | *"Out of 10? Be harsh."* | **Out of 10? Be harsh 👇** |

**Length:** 30–40 seconds.

**Caption:**
> Rate my split out of 10 👇 Be harsh.
>
> [4] days a week at 5:30am: [Push / Pull / Legs / Upper]. Not sure I'm doing enough [for arms / legs].
>
> Built and tracked in Setmodo. And if you like it, you can copy it straight into yours.
>
> #workoutsplit #pushpulllegs #gymlife #strengthtraining #ratemysplit

**Follow-up:** reply to the best comments with "Reply with reel" (*"Loads of you said I need more legs… fine"*). That's a free second video.

**Check:** only mention copying someone's split if sharing a split with a link works the way you'd show it. It does in the app today.

## D. "What members have asked for" (build in public)

**The idea:** show it's real, growing and listening. It gives people a reason to try it, because they can shape it, and it sets up the friends feature.

**Film:** at home or on a walk. Calm and honest.

| Moment | Say it your way | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening line** (word for word) | *"[X] people are using my app now, and here's what they've asked for."* | **[X] people use my app. Here's what they want.** |
| Point 1 | How that number feels for something one person built. Keep it short and genuine. | |
| Point 2 | Request 1 (a real one). Say whether it's done, coming, or "thinking about it". | *1. [request]* |
| Point 3 | Request 2: **friends**. See what your mates trained, copy their split, off unless you turn it on. Say you're building it next. | *2. Friends (building it next)* |
| Point 4 (optional) | Something someone asked for that you're not doing, and why. Honesty works. | *3. [request] (not yet…)* |
| **End question** | *"What would you add?"* | **What would you add? 👇** |

**Length:** 30–45 seconds.

**Caption:**
> [X] people now use an app I built on my own 🤯 Here's what they've asked for:
>
> 1. [real request]
> 2. Friends: see what your mates trained and copy their split (building this next)
> 3. [real request]
>
> What would you add? Every comment gets read 👇
>
> #buildinpublic #gymapp #indiedev #smallbusinessuk #gymlife

**Before filming, fill in:**
- **[X]:** the number of members. Check Admin and use a real number. Round down, never up.
- **Real requests:** anything members have messaged you about, or features you've been asked about. If you can't think of one, use "supersets" (it's on the list) or swap the opening line for *"Here's what I'm building next, and I need your opinion."*

## Suggested order

| When | Video |
|---|---|
| Next training morning | **A. 5:30am brain**, plus the Part 2 story afterwards |
| Following training days | **B. 5:30am club · Day 1, 2, 3…** (2–3 a week) |

| After the reel numbers come in | **D. What members have asked for** |

A and B are filmed on mornings you're already at the gym, so they take no extra time. C and D are one home session.
