# Setmodo content playbook

The single reference for Setmodo's social content and ads: the rules, the style, what's been posted and what worked. **Keep this up to date** whenever something is agreed, posted or learned.

Other files in this folder:

- [`scripts.md`](scripts.md): the earlier word-for-word scripts (kept for ideas; Ben found full scripts didn't sound natural)

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

## 1. Ground rules

- **Never mention NotchLift** (the old name) in anything new: on screen, in captions or in ads. It gives the other app free advertising. Say "Setmodo", or "my app".
- **Talking to camera:** it's FaceTime with one mate who goes to the gym. Look at the lens, say "you" (never "you guys"), and talk normally.
- **Talking points, not scripts.** Only the opening line (the hook) is said word for word. The rest is said in Ben's own words.
- **Only claim things that are true.** Use real member numbers (round down), real requests and real stories. Ben trains at **5:30am**.
- **Every post ends with a question**, and every comment gets a reply, ideally within the first hour.

## 3. Visual style

**Brand colours:** dark `#131416`, lime `#C3ED89`, light text `#EAEBED`. The wordmark is **setmodo.** with a lime full stop.

**Text on talking videos** (mock-up: "It's just me" text-on-screen):
1. **Hook (0–3 s):** 3–6 words, white on a dark box, in the top third. In Instagram's text tool, tap the boxed **A** until the text has a solid background.
2. **Captions:** auto-captions, white and bold with a black outline or shadow, **over the chest, never the face**. 3–5 words at a time.
3. **One lime word per video**, for the key phrase (e.g. "add weight", "zero thinking").
4. **End question:** the same style as the hook, held for 3–4 seconds.
5. **Keep text out of Instagram's areas:** the bottom quarter (username and caption), the right edge (like and comment buttons) and the top strip.
6. At most 2 fonts and 2 colours, and no sticker clutter.

**Covers:** a frame where Ben is at eye level and looking at the lens. Hook in dark boxes at the top, with a lime word. A small "Why I built setmodo."-style line in a dark box near the bottom. Keep everything inside the middle 3:4 area so it still works in the profile grid crop.

## 4. Filming

- **Camera:** front camera, portrait, at **eye level** (propped on books or the dashboard). Filming from below shows the ceiling light and doesn't flatter you.
- **Light:** face a window, or the brightest wall in the gym.
- **Sound:** wear **AirPods**, which work well (Settings → Bluetooth → AirPods → Microphone: Automatic). Avoid echoey rooms; a car with the windows shut is very good. CapCut's **Reduce noise** can fix older clips. If you want an upgrade later, a DJI Mic Mini (about £50–£80).
- **Length:** aim for 25–40 seconds. Cut every pause and start on the first word. Jump cuts are fine.
- **Before filming:** send yourself a 20-second voice note answering the prompt, then film straight away.
- **Batch it:** change your top between videos so they look like different days.
- **Mornings:** film in the car at 5:20am before training and at about 6:45am after. It costs no extra time.

## 5. Formats that work

- **Excuses reel** ("Gym excuses, sorted."): Ben's favourite and the best-performing ad. Caption: *"Which excuse have you used? Be honest 😅👇"*
- **"Help us say goodbye"**: an image that looks like a note announcing that the excuse "I can't remember what I lifted last week" is being retired. Ben likes it.
- **Hero image**: the original best performer under the old name; rebranded for Setmodo.
- **Carousels**: "3 mistakes stopping you getting stronger", "5 signs you're stuck on a plateau". Hook slide, one point per slide, then a CTA slide with the App Store badge and "📌 Save this".
- **Talking to camera**: see `talking-points.md`. Current favourites are **A. 5:30am brain**, **B. 5:30am club · Day N** (a repeatable series), **C. Rate my split** and **D. What members have asked for**.
- **Reply reels**: tap Reply on a comment, then **Reply with reel**.

**Message pillars** (what every piece comes back to):
- Last time's numbers beside every set ("Was it 60 or 62.5?")
- It tells you when to add weight (rep range → target hit → go heavier)
- No thinking needed (5:30am brain)
- Made by one person in Norfolk who reads every message

**Hashtag bank:** #progressiveoverload #gymlife #strengthtraining #gymtips #gymmotivation #buildinpublic #smallbusinessuk #gymapp #5amclub #morningworkout #workoutsplit #pushpulllegs (about 5 per post).

## 6. Ads (Meta)

**Current campaign:** `NL – Sign-ups – Test 4`, objective **Sales**, conversion event **Complete registration** (website pixel).

| Ad set | Budget | Creative | utm_content |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK 18-44 – excuses | £10/day | Excuses video (9:16 + 4:5) | `excuses` |
| UK 18-44 – hero | £10/day | Hero image (4:5 / 9:16) | `hero` |
| UK 18-44 – goodbye | £10/day | "Help us say goodbye" image | `goodbye` |

- **Budget sharing between ad sets:** off.
- **Audience:** Advantage+ audience, UK, 18–44. Interest suggestions: powerlifting, physical fitness, weight training, bodybuilding. **"Employers: Gym" removed**, because it targets gym staff.
- **Call to action:** **Sign up** (not "Learn more").
- **Link:** `https://www.setmodo.com/add-weight?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=app-launch&utm_content={excuses|hero|goodbye|launch}`

**Ad copy:**
- **Excuses:** *"Which excuse have you used? 😅 Setmodo puts last session's weights beside every set and tells you when it's time to go heavier. Works offline. 14 days free, no card."* Headline: *Stop guessing your weights*
- **Launch:** *"Was it 60 or 62.5 last week? 🤔 Setmodo shows your last numbers beside every set and tells you when you've earned more weight. Now on the App Store, and in your browser."* Headline: *Know exactly what to lift next*
- **Goodbye:** *"We're retiring the excuse "I can't remember what I lifted last week." 💔 Setmodo shows your last numbers beside every set. 14 days free."* Headline: *Never forget a lift again*

**What we've learned:**
- Meta put nearly all the spend on the **excuses video**. Test 3: £23.35 for 2 sign-ups (£11.68 each). Test 4, day 1: £8.50 for 1 sign-up.
- Even with separate ad sets, Meta favours one ad. This is **auction overlap**: the ad sets target the same people, so Meta enters only the one it predicts will do best in each auction.
- Test 4, day 2 (11 Nov, full day): excuses got **50 visits and 2 sign-ups for £12.78** (a 4% sign-up rate, **£6.39 per sign-up**: the best so far).
- **Test running 12–13 Nov:** excuses **paused** so hero and goodbye get spend (£10/day each). **Switch excuses back on Saturday 14 Nov.** Then compare cost per sign-up: keep anything at about £10 or less. (A separate campaign wouldn't help: auction overlap works across the whole ad account. For proper tests of several ads, use Meta's A/B test.) Clicks but no sign-ups means the ad and landing page don't match. Few clicks means images lose to video, so test another video next.
- *Original plan (superseded by the test above):* leave it until **Saturday**. If hero and goodbye are still near £0, **pause excuses for Saturday and Sunday** so they get shown, then compare cost per sign-up (aim for about £10 or less) and keep the winners. The proper alternative is Meta's **A/B test** (Experiments, on a computer).
- **Next creative to test:** another **video**, probably the talking-to-camera reel with the best watch time.
- **Log spend** in Setmodo Admin → Ad spend. Admin's funnel shows App Store taps and sign-ups by platform.
