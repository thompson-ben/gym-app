## 0. Quality standard (read this first, every time)

**The bar is the 27 Nov "Notes app" reel and cover** (the versions Ben rated "a lot better"). Their source files are in [`templates/`](templates/). **Start from them**, and change the idea, words and screenshots, rather than starting from a blank page.

### How that post went from boring to good
| | First version (boring) | Rebuilt version (good) |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | A static headline on a dark screen | The problem **happening**: a messy Notes log typed live, a shake, 🤷. Hook on screen from frame 1 |
| The app | A drawn, pretend app | The **real screenshot**, zoomed into "Previous", with a lime ring and a "← last time, already there" sticker |
| Frame | Half empty | Filled edge to edge at every moment |
| Pace | One change every few seconds | Something changes every 0.5–1.5 s |
| Payoff | None | A swipe with a flash from white Notes to the dark app, a zoom to 🎯 Target hit, then "next time: go heavier" |
| Ending | Plain | A full lime card: "Delete the note. 🗑️" plus "Comment APP for the link 👇" |
| Cover | A frame from the video | A dedicated **before / after** cover (see below) |

**The cover went through three versions:**
1. A video frame: not scroll-stopping.
2. A meme style (a hand-drawn red ✕, tilted text boxes, emoji): it stopped the scroll but looked cheap.
3. **The professional version (the standard):** a small uppercase eyebrow, a big left-aligned headline in white → grey → lime, a white Notes card with neat red strikethroughs ("BEFORE"), and the real app in a phone frame with a soft lime glow and a lime ring on the key number ("SETMODO" chip). The wordmark sits top-left, with no emoji and no hand-drawn marks.

### The recipe for every reel
1. **One idea, shown as problem → fix.** The problem in a real-life texture (Notes, iMessage, a lock screen, a whiteboard), then the real app as the fix.
2. **Frame 1 is already interesting:** the problem or the joke, with the hook on screen.
3. **The real app only.** Screenshots from `public/landing/` and `docs/screenshots/`, **zoomed in** on the one number that matters, with a lime ring and a short sticker. Never draw a pretend app.
4. **Fill the frame, and keep it moving.** No more than a third of the screen empty. Something changes every 0.5–1.5 s.
5. **One payoff moment** the viewer waits for: a swipe and flash, a zoom, a ring popping on.
6. **End on a bold lime card** with one action.
7. **12–15 seconds.** Write the caption so it works without sound, and tell Ben the sound vibe to add.

### The recipe for covers and carousels (professional, not meme)
- An eyebrow, a three-tone headline, and real objects (app screens in a phone frame, a Notes card) with chips like "BEFORE" / "SETMODO".
- No hand-drawn marks, tilted boxes or emoji on covers and carousels.
- Check the grid crop: everything important between y 240 and 1680.

### Score it before sending (redo anything under 4)
| Check | 1 (fail) → 5 (great) |
|---|---|
| Would someone stop scrolling in the first second? | |
| Is it obviously about a real gym moment? | |
| Is the real app shown, and is the key number clear? | |
| Does every second have movement or new information? | |
| Does it look like a professional brand (covers and carousels clean)? | |
| Is there one clear action at the end? | |

Include the scores in the message to Ben, one line each, so he can see the reasoning.

## 1. Ground rules

- **Never mention NotchLift** (the old name) in anything new: on screen, in captions or in ads. It gives the other app free advertising. Say "Setmodo", or "my app".
- **Talking to camera:** it's FaceTime with one mate who goes to the gym. Look at the lens, say "you" (never "you guys"), and talk normally.
- **Talking points, not scripts.** Only the opening line (the hook) is said word for word. The rest is said in Ben's own words.
- **Only claim things that are true.** Use real member numbers (round down), real requests and real stories. Ben trains at **5:30am**.
- **Not a fitness influencer.** Ben shares how he trains and why he built the app, but doesn't position himself as a coach. Tips are fine when they lead back to the app.
- **Comment keyword:** captions use *"Comment APP and I'll send you the link"*. Ben replies to the comment ("Sent! 👊") and DMs the link. Use one keyword everywhere so people learn it.
- **Every post ends with a question**, and every comment gets a reply, ideally within the first hour.

## 3. Visual style

**Brand colours:** dark `#131416`, lime `#C3ED89`, light text `#EAEBED`. The wordmark is **setmodo.** with a lime full stop.

**Text on talking videos** (mock-up: "It's just me" text-on-screen):
1. **Hook (0–3 s):** 3–6 words, white on a dark box, in the top third. In Instagram's text tool, tap the boxed **A** until the text has a solid background.
2. **Captions:** auto-captions, white and bold with a black outline or shadow, **over the chest, never the face**. 3–5 words at a time.
3. **One lime word per video**, for the key phrase (e.g. "add weight", "zero thinking").
4. **End question:** the same style as the hook, held for 3–4 seconds.
5. **Keep text out of Instagram's areas:** the bottom quarter (username and caption), the right edge (like and comment buttons) and the top strip.
6. At most 2 fonts and 2 colours, and no sticker clutter.

**Covers:** a frame where Ben is at eye level and looking at the lens. Hook in dark boxes at the top, with a lime word. A small "Why I built setmodo."-style line in a dark box near the bottom. Keep everything inside the middle 3:4 area so it still works in the profile grid crop.

## 4. Filming

- **Camera:** front camera, portrait, at **eye level** (propped on books or the dashboard). Filming from below shows the ceiling light and doesn't flatter you.
- **Light:** face a window, or the brightest wall in the gym.
- **Sound:** wear **AirPods**, which work well (Settings → Bluetooth → AirPods → Microphone: Automatic). Avoid echoey rooms; a car with the windows shut is very good. CapCut's **Reduce noise** can fix older clips. If you want an upgrade later, a DJI Mic Mini (about £50–£80).
- **Length:** aim for 25–40 seconds. Cut every pause and start on the first word. Jump cuts are fine.
- **Before filming:** send yourself a 20-second voice note answering the prompt, then film straight away.
- **Batch it:** change your top between videos so they look like different days.
- **Mornings:** film in the car at 5:20am before training and at about 6:45am after. It costs no extra time.

## 10. Making it interesting (read before every set)

The test for every post: **would a gym-goer stop scrolling, and would they send it to a mate?** If not, change it before sending. A post that's accurate but flat is a fail.

### Creative process (each day)
1. Write **5 different concepts** for the reel and **3 for the carousel**. Use different formats from the list below, not 5 versions of one idea.
2. Score each one from 1 to 5 on: **Stops the scroll** (first 1 second), **Relatable** (a gym moment they've lived), **Emotion** (funny, a bit annoying, satisfying, surprising) and **Shareable** ("tag your gym buddy").
3. Make the highest scorer. Only make a "tip" post if it scores as high as a funny or relatable one.
4. The app appears as **the answer** at the end, not the subject. Lead with the person's problem or the joke.

### Hook formulas (use a different one each day)
- **Callout:** "If you've ever scrolled your Notes app mid-set…"
- **Contrarian:** "You don't need a new programme."
- **POV:** "POV: you finally beat last week's bench"
- **Number:** "3 signs you've plateaued (2 is sneaky)"
- **Confession:** "I skipped legs for 6 months. Here's what happened."
- **Me vs me:** "Me at 5:29am vs me at 5:31am"
- **Mistake:** "Stop doing this between sets"
- **Challenge:** "Beat one number this week. Just one."
- **Question:** "Be honest: do you actually track your lifts?"
- **Gym-buddy bait:** "Send this to the mate who always 'feels strong'"

### Formats to rotate (no filming needed)
- **Text-message chat:** an animated iMessage-style chat between gym mates ("what did you bench last week?" "no idea 😂"), ending on the app.
- **Notes app:** a messy Notes-app workout log that turns into the clean Setmodo screen.
- **Tier list:** "Gym excuses tier list", "Rest times tier list" (S to F).
- **This or that:** two options per slide, with "comment your answers".
- **Me vs me:** a split screen of two moments (Monday vs Friday, 5:29 vs 5:31).
- **Lock-screen notification** (as on 23 Nov), a **leaderboard of your own lifts**, a **progress chart drawing itself**.
- **Myth vs fact** cards, **"Things only 5am lifters understand"** lists, and the **"Help us say goodbye"** note style.
- **Cheat sheets** people save: rep ranges, rest times, splits by days a week.

### Motion and look
- Something **moves in the first 0.5 seconds**. Cut every 1 to 2 seconds. Text appears word by word or line by line, never all at once.
- **Not every post on the dark template.** At least 2 days a week, use a bold **lime background with dark text**, or a light "Notes app" or "iMessage" look, so the grid has variety.
- Emoji and reaction faces are fine for humour. Keep **one big idea per screen**.
- Tell Ben to add a **trending sound** in Instagram (reels without sound get less reach). Suggest the vibe: upbeat, funny or calm.

### Make people respond
- Every post asks for **one specific action**: "Comment APP", "Tag your gym buddy", "Comment 1, 2 or 3", or "Save this".
- Stories rotate stickers: **poll**, **quiz**, **emoji slider** ("How sore are you today?"), **question box** ("Ask me anything about the app"), **"Add Yours"** ("Show your 5am gym"), and **countdown** (for app updates).
- Run **series** people can follow: "5:30am club · Day N", "Gym excuses tier list part 2", "Myth Monday".

### Real material from Ben (use whenever available)
- Ben's real lifts and sessions beat anything invented. When he shares one ("bench 70 × 8, up 1 rep"), make it the day's "real numbers" post.
- Real gym clips and photos (B-roll) go in `docs/media/broll/` when Ben sends them. Prefer them over drawings.

### Lessons from real posts (what "boring" looked like, and the fix)
**27 Nov trial reel "Your Notes app is not a training log": good idea, boring execution.** Ben's verdict. What was wrong:
1. **Static text on a dark screen** for the first seconds. Nothing happened.
2. **Half the frame was empty.** Big blank areas look unfinished.
3. **A drawn, pretend app** instead of the real screenshots. It looked generic.
4. **No "moment":** no reveal, no payoff, nothing to react to.

**The rebuilt version** (same idea, much better) did this:
- **Showed the problem rather than saying it.** A light iPhone Notes screen with a messy workout being **typed live** ("bench 60x8?? or 62.5", "triceps… forgot to write 😬", "what did I do last week 😩"), a shake, and a 🤷 pop-in. The hook sits on screen from frame one.
- **Contrast and a transition:** a white Notes app swiped away to the dark Setmodo app with a flash. Light to dark makes the change feel like a fix.
- **The real app, zoomed in:** the real screenshot (`public/landing/target-hit.png`), zooming into the Previous column, with a lime ring and a sticker "← last time, already there". Then a zoom to "🎯 Target hit", with "next time: go heavier".
- **A punchy ending that's an action:** a full lime screen saying "Delete the note. 🗑️" plus "Comment APP for the link 👇".
- About 14 s, something changing every 0.5–1.5 s.

**Rules from this (apply to every reel):**
- **Never draw a pretend app.** Use the real screenshots, zoomed and highlighted. If the screenshots can't be found, say so instead of drawing one.
- **Fill the frame.** No more than about a third of the screen empty at any moment.
- **Frame 1 shows a problem or a joke,** not a title card.
- **Every reel has a payoff moment:** a zoom, a ring, a pop or a swipe the viewer is waiting for.
- **Lead with real-life textures** (Notes app, iMessage, a lock screen, a gym whiteboard), then reveal the app as the fix.
- **End on a bold full-colour card** with one action (lime background, dark text).

**Covers (from the same post):** a plain frame of text on dark isn't scroll-stopping. What worked was a **diagonal split: the problem versus the fix**. The top half was the messy white Notes app with a big red hand-drawn ✕. The bottom half was the real dark app, zoomed in on "Previous" with a lime ring and a "last time ✓" sticker. Across the middle, huge white-on-black hook text, slightly tilted, with one word in red ("NOT"). Make a dedicated cover image rather than a frame from the video, and check it in the 3:4 grid crop.

**Brand level: professional, not meme.** Ben wants Setmodo to look like a professional brand. The second version of that cover is the standard to follow:
- A small uppercase **eyebrow** line ("STILL LOGGING LIFTS IN NOTES?").
- A big, tight, **left-aligned headline** in three tones: white, then grey, then lime for the key words.
- Two clean **"before" and "after" objects**: a white Notes card with neat red strikethroughs, and the real app in a proper phone frame with a soft lime glow and a lime ring on the key number.
- Small **chips** ("BEFORE" and "SETMODO").
- The wordmark top-left.

**No** hand-drawn crosses, tilted text boxes or emoji on covers. Emoji are fine sparingly inside fun reels; covers and carousels stay clean and premium.
