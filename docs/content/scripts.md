# Talking to camera: 8 FaceTime scripts

> Earlier word-for-word scripts. Ben found full scripts didn't sound natural, so new ideas use the talking-points format in [`talking-points.md`](talking-points.md). Script 1 and the captions are still useful.

**The rule for every one:** you're on FaceTime with one mate who goes to the gym. Their camera is off. Look into the lens, not at yourself on screen. Say "you", never "you guys". Talk the way you'd talk to them.

## How to film them

- **Batch them.** Film 3 or 4 in one go. Change your top between scripts so they look like different days.
- **Phone:** front camera, portrait, at eye level (propped on books, a shelf or a gym bench). Hold it at arm's length for the "walking" ones.
- **Light:** face a window. In the gym, face the brightest wall.
- **Sound:** AirPods mic if you have them. In the gym, film in a quiet corner or the changing room.
- **Don't learn the words.** Read the beats, put the phone down, and say them your way. Do 2 or 3 takes and keep the most natural one, not the most polished.
- **The first 2 seconds decide everything.** Start mid-sentence, already talking. No "Hi everyone" and no "So today I want to…".
- **Captions on**, using auto-captions in Instagram or Edits. Most people watch with the sound off.
- **Length:** 20–40 seconds.
- **End with a question** you actually want answered, then reply to every comment within the hour.

**Never mention the other app's name.** "Setmodo" only, or just "my app".

## 1. "60 or 62.5?"
**Where:** at home or in the gym, phone in hand. **Around 25 seconds.**

| Shot | You say | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| Close-up, holding your phone, a bit lost | "You've got the bar loaded and you can't remember what you did last week." | *60 or 62.5?* |
| Scroll a Notes app workout on camera | "So you're scrolling through your Notes. Between sets. Again." | |
| Back to you | "And then you just guess. And guessing is how you lift the same weight for six months." | *Guessing = no progress* |
| Lean in a little | "That was me. So I built an app that puts last week's numbers right next to every set." | |
| Screen recording: a set with last time beside it (2–3 s) | *(silent)* | *Last time: 60 × 10* |
| Back to you, relaxed | "It's called Setmodo, it's on the App Store. But honestly, what do you track your lifts with?" | *Notes? Paper? Vibes? 👇* |

**Caption:**
> Was it 60 or 62.5? 🤔
>
> If you've ever scrolled your Notes app between sets trying to remember, I built this for you. Setmodo is on the App Store now 📲
>
> What do you track with right now? 👇
>
> #gymlife #progressiveoverload #strengthtraining #gymtips #benchpress

## 3. "The only rule that matters"
**Where:** in the gym, by a rack or bench. **Around 30 seconds.** This is pure value and doesn't sell.

| Shot | You say | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| Walking towards the camera, already talking | "If you only remember one thing about the gym, make it this." | *The only rule that matters* |
| Stop, look at the lens | "Do a bit more than last time. That's it. One more rep. Or a tiny bit more weight." | *Beat last time* |
| Same | "Not a new programme every Monday. Not the perfect split. Just: did I beat last week?" | |
| Hold up two fingers | "Do that for a year and you'll be unrecognisable. Miss it and you'll be lifting the same weights next Christmas." | |
| Same, lighter | "That's literally why my app shows last time beside every set. So you always know the number to beat." | |
| Back to you | "What's a lift you've been stuck on for ages? Tell me and I'll tell you what I'd do." | *Stuck on a lift? 👇* |

**Caption:**
> The only rule that matters in the gym: beat last time 📈
>
> One more rep or a tiny bit more weight. Every week. That's progressive overload, and it beats any fancy programme.
>
> Tell me a lift you're stuck on and I'll reply with what I'd do 👇
>
> #progressiveoverload #gymtips #strengthtraining #gymmotivation #liftingtips

**Why it's worth doing:** the comments give you a week of reply videos (see 8).

## 4. "Reps first, then weight"
**Where:** in the gym, between sets. **Around 30 seconds.**

| Shot | You say | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| Slightly out of breath, close-up | "Quick one, because I see this every time I'm in here." | |
| Same | "Someone does 60 for 8, then next week jumps to 65 and gets 4. Form goes. Ego's happy, progress isn't." | *60 × 8 → 65 × 4 ❌* |
| Look at the lens | "Here's what I do. Pick a range, say 8 to 12. Stay on the same weight until you hit 12 on every set. Then add weight, and you're back at 8." | *8 → 12, then add weight* |
| Screen recording: target range and "Target hit" (2 s) | *(silent)* | *Target hit ✓ Add weight* |
| Back to you | "My app does that bit for you. It tells you when you've earned the jump." | |
| Same | "Do you go up in weight every week, or wait until you've hit the reps?" | *Which are you? 👇* |

**Caption:**
> Stop jumping weight every week 🛑
>
> Pick a rep range (say 8–12). Same weight until you hit the top on every set. Then go heavier and start again at the bottom. Slow, steady, and it actually works.
>
> Setmodo tells you when you've earned the jump.
>
> Weight every week, or reps first? 👇
>
> #doubleprogression #gymtips #hypertrophy #strengthtraining #progressiveoverload

## 5. "The excuse I used for years"
**Where:** in the car in the gym car park, or at home in your kit. **Around 25 seconds.** It's the founder version of the excuses reel, which is your best performer.

| Shot | You say | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| Car seat, seatbelt on, honest | "Can I tell you the excuse I used for years?" | *My excuse for years* |
| Same | "'I'll go tomorrow.' Every time. And tomorrow was always busier than today." | *"I'll go tomorrow"* |
| Same, slower | "What changed it wasn't motivation. It was knowing exactly what I was doing when I walked in. Which workout. Which numbers to beat." | |
| Same | "No plan, no thinking, just go." | |
| Undo seatbelt, reach for the door | "Anyway, I'm going in. What's yours? What's the excuse you use most?" | *What's yours? 👇* |

**Caption:**
> "I'll go tomorrow." My excuse for years 😅
>
> Motivation didn't fix it. Having a plan did: walk in knowing exactly what to do and what numbers to beat.
>
> Be honest, what's the excuse you use most? 👇
>
> #gymmotivation #noexcuses #gymlife #fitnessjourney #discipline

## 7. "What I learned launching an app"
**Where:** at home or on a walk. **Around 35 seconds.**

| Shot | You say | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| Walking, phone at arm's length | "So I launched an app last week. Here's what nobody tells you." | *Launching an app: what nobody tells you* |

**Caption:**
> I launched an app last week. 3 things nobody tells you 👇

>

>
> #buildinpublic #startupuk #founder #appstore #smallbusinessuk

## 8. "Reply to a comment" (repeatable format)
**Where:** anywhere. **Around 25 seconds.** Use it whenever a good comment comes in (script 3 is built to create them).

How to film: in Instagram, tap **Reply** on the comment → **Reply with reel**. The comment sticker appears on your video automatically.

| Shot | You say |
|---|---|
| Comment sticker on screen, you looking at the lens | "[Name] said they've been stuck on 80 kg bench for months. Here's exactly what I'd do." |
| Same | Your answer in 2 or 3 steps. Keep it simple: drop to 70, get 12 reps, build back up 2.5 kg at a time. |
| Same | "Try that for 6 weeks and tell me how it goes." |

**Why it's worth doing:** reply reels are the easiest content to make, the algorithm likes them, and people comment more when they see you answer.
